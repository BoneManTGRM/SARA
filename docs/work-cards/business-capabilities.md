## Evidence and authority limits

Positive local tests use explicitly synthetic independent OWNER_OBSERVED records through the authenticated kernel evidence ingress. They do not claim real customer completion, market demand, deployment, CI or production runtime behavior. Production acceptance requires the exact PRODUCTION evidence grade and exact deployment identity; owner-observed or CI evidence cannot satisfy it. Productization requires accepted-job required capability identities to match its proposed capability inventory, currently qualified enabled contracts, and demonstrated applicable procedure evidence. It produces a candidate only, without prices or automation percentages unsupported by accounting evidence.

Business experiment registration persists the complete design digest in an existing immutable kernel receipt. Evaluation requires that receipt to precede the declared start and the independent result capture to follow it, with exact design/result value binding. Predicted revenue remains separate; it never populates actual revenue.

Parent integration owns CI, exact revisions and production-safe runtime qualification. No consequential external authority was added.
