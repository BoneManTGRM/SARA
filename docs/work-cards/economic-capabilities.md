# Economic calculation capabilities

Use deterministic integer micro-USD arithmetic for supplied financial amounts. Preserve missing values as unknown, predicted versus realized revenue, direct cash versus allocation versus modeled labor, and source identities. Reject duplicate/conflicting ledger IDs and overflow. Ranking is advisory: permissions and obligations precede value; a score never authorizes work or raises a budget.

Implement economic-value-of-work, quote-margin-guard, resource-budget-planner, revenue-opportunity-ranker, profitability-accountant and service-pricing-engine through existing registered contracts and durable invocation receipts. Pricing must identify incomplete evidence and all assumptions. Controlled fixtures must prove probability arithmetic, minimum margin, resource ceilings, missing values, unauthorized high-profit exclusion, obligation priority, and actual/predicted separation.
