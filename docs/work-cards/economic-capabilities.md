## Scope completion contract

Pricing accepts optional bounded market observations tied to a service, scope digest and billing unit. Each observation identifies its source, supplied versus independently evidenced basis, and exact quantified value. Comparable competitor prices, observed willingness to pay and an explicitly supplied value-capture fraction constrain a conditional range above the cost floor. Complexity is an explicit incremental cash estimate, never a fabricated multiplier. Counterevidence prevents a supported demand conclusion. Missing, stale or falsely claimed verified records cannot influence the recommendation. Historical reference-only inputs retain their prior floor behavior. Draft recommendations do not authorize offers.

Work value adds an optional exact work ID and keeps customer value, strategic value, repeatability, deadlines and resource demand in separate units. Ranking applies authority, prerequisites and obligations first, then a declared lexicographic dimension order (default money, deadline, customer value, strategic value, repeatability, resource demand). Unknown values rank after known ones; no points or time are converted to fabricated dollars. Explicit work IDs must match the ranked opportunity. Backward-compatible input schemas retain existing supplied arithmetic cases.

Acceptance: RED cases must prove market evidence can change a recommendation, incompatible or stale evidence is excluded, a market ceiling below required margin blocks, configured nonfinancial dimensions change eligible ordering without bypassing obligations or authority, and exact work identity survives the result. Focused property/arithmetic and frozen qualification regressions remain required.

The shared ledger is read only; no fabricated financial attestation or parallel accounting store is created.
