# Evidence-backed public proof for the $149 founding pilot

Status: SHADOW candidate · owner review required · no customer data processed · no publication or delivery

## Opportunity

The Public Repository Readiness Snapshot has a qualified intake, immutable public evidence, bounded analysis, a deterministic report gate, and owner-controlled delivery. Its remaining commercial proof gap is a safe way to turn a completed pilot or an unpaid demonstration into a concise public proof card without inventing a customer, hiding commercial terms, quoting someone without consent, or implying a security guarantee.

The US Federal Trade Commission says advertising claims must be truthful, non-deceptive, and evidence-based, and its current review and testimonial guidance addresses fake or false testimonials and disclosure of material connections:

- [Advertising and Marketing Basics](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing)
- [Endorsements, Influencers, and Reviews](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews)
- [The Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule: Questions and Answers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)

Those sources establish the need for conservative, evidence-backed public claims. They do not prove demand for SARA's service or validate the $149 price.

## Candidate

`compilePublicPilotProof` converts verified completion facts into one owner-review card. It:

1. pins every evidence URL to the same public repository and exact 40-character commit;
2. binds the card to a SHA-256 report digest;
3. clearly distinguishes an unpaid demonstration from a customer case study;
4. requires completed owner review, confirmed customer delivery, verified commercial terms, and customer publication consent before a customer card can pass;
5. withholds a customer name unless naming permission is confirmed;
6. requires separate testimonial publication consent and discloses whether the service was paid, discounted, or free;
7. blocks incomplete reports, missing review categories, moving citations, cross-repository evidence, and unsupported assurance claims; and
8. always returns `externalPublicationAuthorized: false`.

The compiler does not collect customer data, contact anyone, publish a page, deliver a report, request payment, or change a repository.

## Falsifiable value

For the first three owner-approved public proof cards, record whether the compiler catches at least one missing consent, unverifiable commercial claim, moving citation, or unsupported assurance before publication. Also measure owner review time. Retain the compiler if it prevents one material public-claim correction while keeping median review time at or below ten minutes; otherwise simplify or retire it.

## Safest next step

After a real owner-approved pilot is completed, pass only the minimum verified facts and immutable public citations into the compiler. The owner must compare every claim to the underlying evidence and separately approve any publication. Until then, use the demonstration mode and label it explicitly as unpaid with no customer involved.
