## Owner-reviewed delivery bundle

A completed pilot may contain:

1. **Scope and source manifest**
   - authorized repository and immutable commit;
   - inspected evidence classes;
   - explicit truncation, omissions, unsupported tools, and evidence gaps.

2. **Repository readiness snapshot**
   - bounded readiness summary;
   - prioritized, evidence-supported findings;
   - immutable source locations;
   - conservative severity and confidence language.

3. **One repair preparation brief**
   - selected from the highest-priority supported finding;
   - exact finding excerpt and source identity;
   - known reproduction steps and proposed acceptance criteria;
   - unknown repository, revision, reproduction, or verification fields left visibly unknown;
   - explicit statement that repair execution, spending, submission, merge, and deployment are not authorized.

4. **Limitations and next decision**
   - what was not assessed;
   - what requires specialist review;
   - the safest owner-controlled next step.

5. **Protected delivery**
   - exact owner-reviewed report digest;
   - existing time-limited delivery mechanism;
   - no claim of recipient acceptance unless separately evidenced.

The repair brief is a planning artifact, not a repair, guarantee, certification, exploit validation, or authorization to modify code.

## Customer-facing description candidate

## First-three-pilot validation

Hypothesis: adding one deterministic repair preparation brief makes the $149 report more actionable without materially increasing scope, cost, or correction risk.

For each genuine eligible paid pilot, record:

| Measure | Pass signal | Fail signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Report corrections before delivery | No material evidence or authority correction | Any unsupported claim reaches owner review |
| Additional owner review time | 30 minutes or less | More than 30 minutes |
| Customer clarification | No confusion that repair work is excluded | Customer reasonably believes code changes are included |
| Brief usefulness | Customer or owner selects a concrete next decision from it | Brief adds no decision beyond the report |
| Actual cost | Remains within existing authorized job ceiling | Any unsupported or unreconciled cost |

Decision after three completed genuine pilots:

- **Retain** the bundle only if all three stay within authority/cost boundaries and at least two briefs produce a concrete next decision.
- **Revise** if usefulness is observed but scope language causes confusion.
- **Remove** the repair brief if it repeatedly adds review burden without a concrete decision.
- Do not infer demand, pricing power, realized revenue, or profitability from fewer than three genuine paid deliveries.
